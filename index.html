AirCoding uses MediaPipe and OpenCV for real-time hand tracking. It's lightweight and runs smoothly on most modern laptops.

📖 How to Use

  1. Launch AirCoding — the app opens a window showing your camera feed.
  2. Calibrate — hold your hand up to the camera. A skeleton of your hand appears once it's detected.
  3. Gestures — use predefined gestures (e.g., pinch to click, swipe to scroll, open palm to stop). The on-screen guide shows available gestures.
  4. Voice — make a specific gesture to start voice input. Speak clearly; your words are typed automatically.
  5. Stop — close the app window or use a gesture to exit.

You can customize gestures in the settings panel (click the gear icon). The app remembers your preferences.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is AirCoding free?

Yes, AirCoding is open-source and free to use. No hidden costs.

Does it work in low light?

Yes, but good lighting improves accuracy. The camera needs to see your hand clearly.

Can I use it with multiple monitors?

Yes, gestures work across all screens. The app tracks your hand position relative to the camera.

Will it work with my laptop's built-in camera?

Absolutely. Any standard webcam works. For best results, use a camera with at least 720p resolution.
